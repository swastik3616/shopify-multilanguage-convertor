1. Subscription Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription to the Language Translator Plugin at any time. To cancel, you must uninstall the App from your Shopify admin dashboard. Once uninstalled, Shopify will immediately cancel your recurring subscription, and you will not be billed in future billing cycles.

Please note that merely deleting the app's code from your theme does not cancel the billing; the App must be formally uninstalled from the Apps section in your Shopify admin.

2. Refund Policy

As a SaaS Shopify application, our costs are tied directly to usage (especially AI translation API costs). However, we want you to be completely satisfied. Our refund policy is as follows:

  • First Charge Refunds: If you are unhappy with the App, you may request a refund within 7 days of your initial subscription charge.
  • Case-by-Case Review: Refunds requested after the 7-day period are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. We generally approve refunds if the App failed to function as described due to a bug on our end, but we do not issue refunds for unused time on a subscription you forgot to cancel.
  • Prorated Refunds: Shopify's billing system does not support automatic prorated refunds. Any approved refunds will be processed manually as app credits or direct refunds via the Shopify Partner Dashboard.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at swastik3616@gmail.com. Please include:

  • Your `*.myshopify.com` URL.
  • The date of the charge.
  • A brief explanation of why you are requesting a refund.

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 1-2 business days.
