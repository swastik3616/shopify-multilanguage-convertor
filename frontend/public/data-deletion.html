At Swastik3616, we respect your privacy and ensure that you retain control over your store's data. This policy outlines how data is handled when you decide to stop using the Language Translator Plugin.

1. Automatic Deletion (Shopify Webhooks)

We are fully compliant with Shopify's mandatory data webhooks. If you uninstall the Language Translator Plugin from your Shopify admin dashboard, Shopify sends a shop/redact webhook payload to our servers exactly 48 hours after uninstallation.

Upon receiving this webhook, our automated systems will permanently delete all database records associated with your store, including API tokens, translated content caches, and merchant account metadata.

2. Manual Deletion Requests

If you or one of your customers requests the deletion of specific personal data before the app is uninstalled, we comply with Shopify's customers/redact webhook. Furthermore, you may directly request manual deletion of your store's account data at any time by emailing us.

To manually request an immediate data purge, email swastik3616@gmail.com with your .myshopify.com domain. We will verify your identity as the store owner and complete the data erasure within 5 business days.

3. Data Backups

To maintain service integrity and disaster recovery capabilities, we maintain secure, encrypted backups of our databases. When your data is deleted from our active production database, it may still exist in our encrypted backup archives for a maximum of 30 days before being automatically overwritten and permanently destroyed.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding our data retention practices, please reach out to us at swastik3616@gmail.com.
