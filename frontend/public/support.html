1. Support Channels

We provide support for the Language Translator Plugin exclusively via email. You can reach our support team at swastik3616@gmail.com. For self-help, please refer to our documentation located at shopify-multilanguage-convertor-plugin.vercel.app.

2. Operating Hours & Response Times

Our standard operating hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (EST). We aim to respond to all technical support inquiries within 24 to 48 hours. Critical bugs affecting store frontend functionality are prioritized and addressed as quickly as possible.

3. What Our Support Covers

Our support team is happy to assist you with:

  • General inquiries about app features and functionality.
  • Help with initial setup and configuration of the translation plugin.
  • Troubleshooting bugs, errors, or unexpected behavior within the App interface.
  • Questions regarding billing, subscription upgrades, or cancellations.

4. What Our Support Does Not Cover

To keep our support efficient, there are certain areas we cannot assist with:

  • Custom Theme Development: We do not provide custom coding, CSS, or Liquid modifications for your specific Shopify theme to change how the language switcher looks, beyond the App's provided settings.
  • Third-Party Apps: We cannot troubleshoot conflicts caused by other third-party Shopify apps installed on your store.
  • General Shopify Support: For general questions about running your Shopify store, please contact Shopify Support.
