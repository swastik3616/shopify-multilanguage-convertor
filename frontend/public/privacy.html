This Privacy Policy describes how Swastik3616 ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you install and use the Language Translator Plugin (the "App") in connection with your Shopify-supported store.

1. Information We Collect

When you install the App, we are automatically able to access certain types of information from your Shopify account required for the App's functionality:

  • Store Information: Your store name, domain, email address, and localization settings.
  • Content Data: Products, collections, themes, and existing translations required to generate multilingual content.
  • Merchant Account Information: Details necessary for billing and subscription verification via Shopify.

We do not sell your personal information, merchant data, or customer data to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to provide and improve the App's services, including:

  • Generating AI-driven translations for your store's content.
  • Communicating with you regarding updates, support, and billing.
  • Ensuring seamless integration with Shopify's APIs and maintaining performance.

3. Third-Party Services & AI Providers

Our App utilizes secure third-party services to function. We share only the strictly necessary text content (e.g., product descriptions) with our trusted AI translation providers (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Groq, and Ollama) to generate localized text. These providers are not permitted to use your store data to train their models or for any other purpose outside of fulfilling the API request.

Our application backend is securely hosted on Render, adhering to industry-standard security practices.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as you have the App installed. We fully comply with Shopify's Mandatory Webhooks:

  • Store Redact (shop/redact): If you uninstall the App, we will purge your store's data from our databases within 48 hours.
  • Customer Redact (customers/redact): If a customer requests the deletion of their data, we will immediately process the deletion of any relevant customer data we may hold (note: our app primarily processes store content, not individual customer data).
  • Customer Data Request (customers/data_request): We will provide any requested customer data stored by our App upon request.

5. GDPR & Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to access the Personal Information we hold about you, port it to a new service, and ask that your Personal Information be corrected, updated, or erased. If you would like to exercise these rights, please contact us at the email provided below.

6. Contact Us

For more information about our privacy practices, if you have questions, or if you would like to make a complaint, please contact us by e-mail at swastik3616@gmail.com.
